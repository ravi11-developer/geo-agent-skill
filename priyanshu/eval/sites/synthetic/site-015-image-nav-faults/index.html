Calder Foods

Calder Foods is a contract manufacturer of shelf-stable sauces, dressings and marinades for retail and food service brands. Founded in 2009, we operate two SQF-certified plants in Ohio and Indiana with a combined capacity of 42,000,000 units per year. Minimum order quantity is 6,000 units per SKU, and typical lead time is 5 weeks from approved formula.

Our team handles formulation, sourcing, hot-fill and cold-fill production, retort processing, labelling and pallet-ready packout. We are audited annually to SQF Level 2 and hold organic and kosher certifications. Current capacity utilisation is 78%, and we are accepting new programmes for 2027 production slots as of September 2026.
