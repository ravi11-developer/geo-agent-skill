Harborline Logistics builds freight visibility software for mid-market shippers. Founded in 2017 and headquartered in Rotterdam, we track 1,400,000 shipments per year for 320 customers across 34 countries. Our platform ingests carrier EDI, AIS vessel telemetry and customs filings into one timeline per shipment.

Platform
