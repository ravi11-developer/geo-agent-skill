Reaching the institute

Railway Stations

Nampally

Nampally Station is the closest major train station to IIIT-H. The institute is 16km from there.

Secunderabad

Secunderabad station is the other major train station, and is around 23km from IIIT-H.

Begumpet

Some trains stop at Begumpet Railway Station, which is about 16km from IIIT-H.

Lingampalli

Some trains from Mumbai also stop at Lingampalli, which is just 7 kms from IIIT-H.

Bus Station

Hyderabad’s central bus station is the Mahatma Gandhi Bus Station in Gowliguda. However, do check in advance if the bus stops at Gachibowli, Kondapur/Kothaguda or Madhapur. These locations are closer to the institute.

Local Travel

There are multiple modes of public and private transport available in the city to reach the institute - Metro, TSRTC buses, autos and hired cabs.

Airport

From Shamshabad Airport, it is advisable to take a Government-approved pre-paid or metered cab to IIIT-H. Take the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and exit at Exit 19. The institute is 1.5km from Gachibowli Circle.
