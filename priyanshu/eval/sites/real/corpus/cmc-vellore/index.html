Who we are

We seek to be a witness to the healing ministry of Christ through excellence in education, service and research.

For discerning patients seeking an expert medical opinion they can genuinely trust, CMC Vellore delivers a wide spectrum of healthcare services under one roof – with integrity, compassion and excellence, the signature of close to 125 years of service in the spirit of Christ. Everyday, over 9000 patients from diverse communities near and far visit CMC Vellore, one of the country’s leading hospitals.
