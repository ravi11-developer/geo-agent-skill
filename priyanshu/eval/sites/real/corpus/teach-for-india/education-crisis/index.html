THE EDUCATION CRISIS

India is facing a crisis of unprecedented proportions. Embedded in the Indian Constitution is a social contract founded on the promise of justice, liberty, equality and fraternity for all Indian citizens. This promise is the bedrock of our nation’s ideals, and still, our systems fail to deliver on this promise for millions of children from low-income communities.

28M

Children in India do not attend school

68%

Students do not make it to college

42%

Students dropout of school before Grade 10

65%

Students in Grade 3 are already behind grade level
We must do more, faster, to change the system.

There is a Solution.

It will take a movement of leaders working together, with others, at all levels of the system to provide an excellent, equitable education to all of India's children. One that will guarantee them liberty, equality, fraternity, and justice - putting them on a fundamentally transformed life path.
