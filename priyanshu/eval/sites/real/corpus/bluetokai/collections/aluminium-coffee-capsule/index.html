      Enjoy Specialty Coffee with Our Convenient Coffee Capsules

      Elevate your daily coffee routine with our coffee capsules, designed for quick, mess-free brewing that brings the taste of India’s finest specialty coffees to your cup. Crafted from high-quality aluminum and nitrogen-flushed to lock in freshness and flavor, these capsules offer the perfect blend of convenience and exceptional quality.

      Brewing Café-Style Coffee at Home Has Never Been Easier

      With our coffee capsules, you can brew café-quality coffee at home in just seconds. Simply insert a capsule into your Nespresso®* or Nespresso®*-compatible machine, press the button, and enjoy a rich, full-bodied espresso. Each capsule delivers the perfect shot of espresso, which can be used as the base for your favourite drinks—whether it’s a bold Americano, a creamy latte, or a frothy cappuccino.

      A Delicious Selection of Coffee Capsules in Four Varieties

      Choose from four delicious variants of our coffee pods, featuring diverse roast profiles from medium to dark, including popular options like Attikan Estate. These capsules are available in packs of 16 or 36, making them ideal for coffee enthusiasts brewing solo or sharing with friends.

      Hassle-Free, High-Quality Coffee Anytime, Anywhere

      Upgrade your coffee experience with our coffee capsules and enjoy the taste of freshly brewed specialty coffee wherever you are—effortlessly.
