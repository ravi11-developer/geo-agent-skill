Frequently Asked Questions, Answered

Qualifying startups get access to Chargebee Billing completely free until you bill your first $1 million in cumulative revenue.

Your startup qualifies if you:

  • Are a new customer to Chargebee (not currently on a paid plan)
  • Are part of a partner Accelerator or VC portfolio network
  • Have raised less than $5 million in funding
We'd still love to work with you. When you fill out the application form on this page, just select the $5M+ funding option and our team will reach out to discuss a custom offer tailored to your stage and scale.
Someone from our team will review your application and reach out to you within 2-3 business days to discuss next steps and get you set up.
Cumulative billing is the total amount you've invoiced to customers through Chargebee over time - not your ARR or MRR at any given moment. For example, if you bill $8K in month one, $12K in month two, and $15K in month three, you've hit $35K in cumulative billing. The free offer continues until your total billing reaches $1 million.
You'll transition to a pay-as-you-go plan with no annual commitment. We'll notify you before you hit your threshold, and work with you to make sure your new plan is the right fit for your needs.
Use the chat on our website or email startups@chargebee.com . We’re here to help.

All pricing references are in USD. For customers outside the US, equivalent thresholds will be determined based on current exchange rates.
