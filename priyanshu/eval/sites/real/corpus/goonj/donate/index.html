Our Recent Impact

More than 110,000+ community projects have been undertaken around water, sanitation, access & infrastructure, agriculture, women health and hygiene, etc.

* For the period 2014 to 2025

More than 20 million lives have been touched across 31 States / UTs in India

* For the period 2014 to 2025

Across the country channelised more than 72 million kgs of material which act as a catalyst for large-scale rural development, dignity and disaster response

* For the period 2014 to 2025

Repurposed more than 9.5 million kgs of cloth surplus into innovative daily utility products, saving millions of kgs from landfills/burning while at the same time generating livelihood all throughout the year.

* For the period 2014 to 2025

In the last 26 years we have worked on more than 40 disasters from earthquakes to floods and Covid to some ignored disasters like winters, fire etc

Guidelines

As per Income Tax guidelines, organizations will have to mandatorily generate certificate 10BE for contributors. For this, contributors must provide their complete address and a Govt recognized unique identification number.

As per the FCRA 2010, to receive from foreign contributors, submission of a passport copy (front and back pages) is mandatory.

All Indian contributions made to GOONJ are eligible for tax exemption of 50% u/s 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
