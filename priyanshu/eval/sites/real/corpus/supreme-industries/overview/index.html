    Founded in 1942,

    Supreme has had a pre-eminent history spanning nearly 80 years in the plastics industry.

    As the largest plastics processors in the nation, we effectively handle volumes of over 670000 metric tons of polymers annually and provide a diverse and comprehensive selection of plastic products in India. Supreme has been home to quality innovation, with the foundation being its mission & vision.

    Vision

    To contribute to the growth of the Indian
    economy through the largest range of
    plastic products.

    Mission

    Being the most value-driven solution provider in all of our plastic product businesses with the objective of adding value to our customers' daily lives and fostering a culture of respect and empowerment for all our stakeholders.

    What makes us Supreme?

    Supreme holds its unparalleled position as a holistic conglomerate because of the way we work towards setting new industry standards, the relationships we forge, and our innovations that impact plenty of lives.

    Pillars of Growth

    Eight decades ago, Supreme set out on a quest for a glorious future. And the only metric for this glory has been growth. What made this possible? Since its inception, Supreme laid the foundation for strong pillars that evolved as the source of strength & growth, helping Supreme propel itself to newer heights.

    Diverse Business Verticals

    With 9 diverse business verticals, Supreme is the largest manufacturer of plastic products with a wide range of applications fulfilling diverse customer needs. Our broad selection of products spans many categories and is known for quality and innovation. We exceed customer expectations through an unmatched product portfolio of more than 15000+ product SKUs.

    Unparalleled Innovations

    Our constant efforts have been focused on product innovation and continuous quality improvement. By keeping a close watch on the market's current needs and trends, our team of committed R&D experts continuously develops new products that improve the quality of life. Additionally, we strive to reduce the carbon footprint by continuously improving our manufacturing processes.

    Revolutionary Manufacturing Plants

    We’re India’s one of the leading plastic processing companies with our manufacturing and distribution roots spread across the country. With the strength of 35 plants and manufacturing facilities, Supreme processes more than 750000 metric tons of plastic polymer each year. Every facility is outfitted with vanguard machinery and storage facilities with our largest facility spanning 130 acres at Gadegaon, Maharashtra. This enables us to serve our supply chain stakeholders better.

    Advanced Upskilling Initiatives

    Our Knowledge Centres, created as part of our Pipe Initiative, provide visiting professionals like plumbers, contractors, consultants and architects with the means to develop their knowledge and skills while also showcasing the product line and its use. Our five knowledge centres are located in Gadegaon, Malanpur, Kharagpur, Ernakulum, and Erode.

    Team

    For Supreme, growth is a journey of companionship. It takes the combined efforts of a group of people who are passionate about accomplishing a shared vision. In our existence of more than 8 decades, Supreme has always been a family of enterprising and optimistic individuals where every member has played a pivotal role in creating a compelling tomorrow.
