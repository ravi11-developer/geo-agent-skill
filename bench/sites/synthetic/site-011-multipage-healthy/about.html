Harborline Logistics

Harborline Logistics was founded in 2017 by two former port operations engineers. The company employs 210 people across Rotterdam, Singapore and Chicago, and serves 320 customers in 34 countries. Revenue reached $41M ARR as of Q2 2026.

Leadership

CEO: Marijke de Vries. CTO: Tomas Alvarez. Both joined at founding and still lead the company as of 2026.

Certifications

Harborline Logistics is SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 27001 certified, audited annually. Contact compliance@harborline.example.com for reports.
