Harborline Logistics

Harborline Logistics is a coastal freight and warehousing company founded in 2011 and headquartered in Savannah, Georgia. Harborline Logistics moves 240,000 containers a year for 610 importers and operates 1.2 million square feet of bonded warehouse space across six Atlantic ports.

Our Services

Coastal Freight Forwarding

Scheduled container service between Savannah, Charleston, Norfolk and Jacksonville with 48-hour port-to-port transit. Rates start at $1,150 per forty-foot container.

Bonded Warehousing

Customs-bonded storage with 24-hour security, climate control and real-time inventory visibility. Rates start at $18 per pallet per month.

Customs Brokerage

Licensed customs brokerage with electronic entry filing and a 4-hour average clearance time. Flat fee of $145 per entry.
