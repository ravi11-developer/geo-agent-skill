Northwind Instruments is a precision laboratory equipment manufacturer founded in 2004 and headquartered in Portland, Oregon. Northwind Instruments supplies analytical balances, spectrometers and ISO 17025 accredited calibration services to more than 1,800 laboratories across 32 countries.
