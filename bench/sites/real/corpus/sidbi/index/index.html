SIDBI celebrated its 37th Foundation Day in the presence of Hon'ble Finance Minister Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman, Shri Sanjay Lohiya, Special Secretary, Department of Financial Services, along with senior dignitaries, policymakers, industry leaders, and stakeholders from the MSME ecosystem

1/6

28/04/2026 The inauguration of SIDBI’s new Branch Office in Raipur by the DMD,SIDBI marks a significant step in strengthening the Bank’s presence in Chhattisgarh and enhancing support to the MSME sector in the region. The office, located at LIC Building, Pandri, will improve accessibility and service delivery for MSMEs across the state. The occasion also saw participation from senior officials, industry representatives, customers, and key stakeholders, reflecting strong engagement with the ecosystem. A Customer Meet was organised alongside the inauguration, underscoring SIDBI’s focus on prompt service delivery and strengthening partnerships to support MSME growth

2/6

02/04/2026 The SIDBI family came together in Mumbai for a memorable Annual Staff Welfare Gathering, thoughtfully organised by the Local Welfare Committee. The evening brought colleagues and their families under one roof, creating a warm and vibrant atmosphere that celebrated the spirit of unity, togetherness, and camaraderie. A special highlight of the evening was the captivating monologue “Kuch Bhi Ho Sakta Hai” by Anupam Kher. His powerful and thought-provoking performance resonated deeply with the audience, leaving everyone inspired, reflective, and energised. The gathering beautifully reflected the true essence of the SIDBI family strengthening bonds, cherishing shared moments, and nurturing a strong sense of belonging that extends far beyond the workplace.

3/6

18/03/2026 The CMD of SIDBI delivered the opening keynote at Capital Connect 2026, organised by TiE Delhi-NCR, on the theme “India’s Growth Decade: The New Architecture of Capital, Innovation & Opportunity.” The address set the tone for the event by presenting a forward-looking perspective on India’s evolving economic landscape and the pivotal role of capital and innovation in shaping the country’s next phase of growth. The keynote highlighted the rapid expansion of India’s startup ecosystem and underscored SIDBI’s catalytic contribution through initiatives such as the Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) and FFS 2.0. These initiatives have played a significant role in accelerating investments across critical sectors including DeepTech, Artificial Intelligence, and Advanced Manufacturing, thereby strengthening the foundation for innovation-led growth. The address further emphasised the need to build a robust and future-ready capital architecture that can unlock long-term opportunities, support emerging enterprises, and drive sustainable economic development. It also aligned this vision with the broader national goal of Viksit Bharat 2047, reinforcing SIDBI’s commitment to enabling a resilient, inclusive, and globally competitive ecosystem.

4/6

09/03/2026 SIDBI was honoured to welcome Sudha Chandran ji as the team came together to celebrate the spirit of womanhood in the presence of the DMD, SIDBI. The occasion was marked by an atmosphere of admiration and inspiration, as her presence added depth and meaning to the celebration. Through her powerful life journey and moving words, she reminded everyone that determination, resilience, and courage can overcome even the most formidable challenges. Her story served as a source of motivation, leaving a lasting impact on all those present. The celebration concluded on a joyful note with a cake-cutting ceremony, bringing the SIDBI family together in a moment of appreciation, unity, and shared celebration.

5/6

28/02/2026 At the Customer Meet held in Ahmedabad, the CMD of SIDBI engaged with MSMEs and key ecosystem partners in a meaningful and constructive dialogue centred on growth, collaboration, and opportunity. The interaction provided a valuable platform for stakeholders to exchange insights, address challenges, and explore pathways to strengthen the MSME ecosystem. The discussions highlighted SIDBI’s progressive initiatives and innovative credit solutions, designed to enhance financial accessibility, empower enterprises, and enable scalable, globally competitive growth. Emphasis was laid on fostering an enabling environment where businesses can thrive through tailored financial support and strategic partnerships. The meet concluded on a positive and forward-looking note, reaffirming SIDBI’s continued commitment to supporting MSMEs in their growth journey and contributing to the vision of building a stronger, resilient, and self-reliant India.
