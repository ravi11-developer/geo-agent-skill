About Us

The World's Largest Indian
Vegetarian Restaurant

Walk into a world where each dosa has a story to tell, every aroma brings back home, and every bite is a celebration of tradition - Welcome to the kitchen of India, world over.

The Familiar Flavour of India, Everywhere.

In a world where food brings people together and cultures and memories, we think that being away shouldn't mean missing a taste of home. Grounded in authenticity and dedicated to perfection, we're the so-called dollar value with a way better than dollar menu. And now, we're a part of daily life for millions of people across the world.
Saravanaa Bhavan - now one of the world's most pure vegetarian restaurant chains. Offering more than 370 dishes made fresh from scratch, and 100+ outlets across 24 countries, with the same tastes, the same styles, the same feelings, the same warmth and the same attention to detail and the highest standards, no matter where you are in the world.

Where Consistency Meets Craft.

Our kitchens run on discipline, cleanliness and accuracy. Each dish is the result of meticulously calibrated procedures - central kitchen facilities, custom spice blends, and highly skilled culinary teams make sure that whether you are eating in Chennai, Chicago, or Qatar, the experience is equally fresh, authentic, and memorable.
For us, excellence isn't a goal - it's the norm. And that's why we keep growing - from fast-service counters and specialty drinks, through our own bakery, house ice cream lines, to our dairy discoveries - every new concept adds one more laye to the Saravanaa Bhavan experience.

Beyond Food, We Serve a Legacy.

At the center is our staff of more than thousands of dedicated employees. But they don't just serve food - they are also the keepers of a legacy. We do not pursue awards - we pursue smiles. Not only do we take our traditions - we perfect them and raise the bar each and every day.
For over 44 years, we've been composing a story without full stops, only new milestones.
Every dish is a tale. Every outlet, a chapter. Each delighted guest is a member of our growing family.

Saravanaa Bhavan began with a simple dream in Chennai and has grown into a global symbol of authentic Indian vegetarian cuisine. With over 44 years of tradition, taste, and trust, we continue to serve delicious food made with passion, discipline, and innovation across the world.
