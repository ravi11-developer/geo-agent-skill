Support Flood-Affected Families in Assam

The devastating floods in Assam have displaced thousands of families, leaving homes, schools, roads and farmlands submerged. As rivers continue to overflow, families are taking shelter in relief camps or temporary shelters, struggling without access to clean drinking water, sanitation, healthcare and food.

For families who have lost almost everything, the need extends beyond rescue. Access to medical care, safe food, hygiene supplies and basic necessities is critical to prevent disease outbreaks and help people recover with dignity.

Why Your Support Matters

The impact of floods doesn't end when the water recedes. Contaminated water, overcrowded relief camps and poor sanitation significantly increase the risk of waterborne and vector-borne diseases, while disruption to local health services leaves many communities without access to timely medical care.

To respond to this humanitarian crisis, Smile Foundation is mobilising its healthcare resources to support flood-affected communities across Assam. Our medical teams will provide primary healthcare services, essential medicines, health consultations and referrals where required, alongside the distribution of hygiene kits and ready-to-eat food to families in need.

Your Donation Can Help Provide

Primary Healthcare: Medical consultation, essential medicines, treatment for common illnesses, health screening

Hygiene Kits: Soaps, toothpaste, sanitary napkins, detergent, disinfectants, mosquito repellent, other daily hygiene essentials

Ready-to-Eat Food: Nutritious meal packets, biscuits, roasted gram, chikki, drinking water, baby food, etc

Together, these interventions help protect families from disease, address urgent health needs and ensure that children, women, elderly people and other vulnerable communities have access to essential support during this difficult time.

Every contribution, big or small, can help save lives, prevent illness and bring hope to families struggling to recover from the floods.

Donate today to support Assam Flood Relief and help provide emergency healthcare, food and humanitarian aid to families in need.

The floods have affected lakhs of people across Assam, disrupting access to shelter, healthcare, food, clean water and sanitation. Immediate humanitarian assistance is essential to help families survive the crisis and begin rebuilding their lives.

Your donation will support Smile Foundation's flood relief efforts by helping provide:
1. Primary healthcare services
2. Essential medicines
3. Hygiene kits
4. Ready-to-eat food
5. Other critical relief support based on the evolving needs of affected communities.

Smile Foundation is mobilising doctors, healthcare professionals and medical resources from multiple ongoing healthcare projects to reach flood-affected communities. The teams will provide primary healthcare, essential medicines, treatment for common illnesses, health screening and referrals wherever necessary.

Floodwaters often contaminate drinking water sources and create conditions that increase the risk of diarrhoea, cholera, skin infections, mosquito-borne diseases and other illnesses. Hygiene kits help families maintain basic sanitation and reduce the risk of disease outbreaks.

Many families have lost access to kitchens, cooking fuel and food supplies. Ready-to-eat food provides immediate nourishment to people living in relief camps or temporary shelters until normal food supplies can be restored.

Relief is prioritised for the most vulnerable families, including children, pregnant and lactating women, elderly persons, persons with disabilities and economically vulnerable households in the worst-affected communities.

Smile Foundation works closely with local communities, district authorities and field teams to assess needs and ensure relief reaches families who need it the most.
