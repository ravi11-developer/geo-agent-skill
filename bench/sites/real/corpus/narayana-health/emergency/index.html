Expert Emergency Care, Anytime You Need It

At Narayana Health, we recognise that medical emergencies can happen anytime without warning and without an appointment. There might be a sudden cardiac arrest, a traumatic injury, as in a car crash, a stroke, a massive internal bleed, a complication of pregnancy, or a child in distress. Whether it's an acute emergency or not, our staff are trained for prompt response.
