What we do

HelpAge’s Programmes for Holistic Elder Care

At every step, we are together – especially when they need us the most.

Healthcare

Helping disadvantaged elders overcome financial and physical barriers to treatment, comfort and well-being.

  1. Primary Healthcare– Bringing doorstep care and treatment
  2. Vision Restoration & Correction– Restoring sight and independence
  3. Mental Health– Supporting mental and emotional well-being
  4. Telehealth– Connecting elders to medical care remotely
  5. Palliative Care– Bringing comfort and dignity
Agecare

Providing support systems that enable older persons to live with dignity, independence and connection.

  1. Elder Helplines– Offering guidance and support
  2. Senior Care Homes– Creating spaces of belonging
  3. Active Ageing Centres– Encouraging connection and participation
  4. Physiocare– Improving mobility and comfort
  5. Caregiving– Supporting care at every step
  6. Mobility Aids– Enabling greater independence
  7. Support a Gran– Sponsoring an elder in need
  8. Elder Help Desk– Hospital navigation and assistance
Livelihoods & Resilience

Helping vulnerable elders rebuild stability, resilience and self-reliance during hardship and crisis.

  1. Elder Self Help Groups– Building financial and social security
  2. Disaster Management– Supporting relief and recovery
Awareness & Advocacy

Driving awareness, inclusion and action for the rights and well-being of older persons.

  1. Institute for Longevity & Ageing Research – Building Dignified Ageing Futures
  2. Awareness & Training Workshops – Empowering with knowledge and guidance
  3. SAVE – Fostering intergenerational values
  4. Policy Advocacy – Strengthening elder rights
  5. Research – Driving change through insights
