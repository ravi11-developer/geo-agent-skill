Energy Solutions

overview

With a legacy of over 165 years of engineering excellence, Greaves delivers dependable power solutions designed to meet diverse operational demands. Our comprehensive portfolio of diesel generator sets, ranging from 5 kVA to 2500 kVA, ensures reliable power across industrial, commercial, and infrastructure applications.

Engineered to meet specific customer requirements and backed by strong aftermarket support, including spares and service, our solutions are built for consistent performance throughout their lifecycle. Combining deep technical expertise, proven engineering expertise & customer-centric approach, Greaves enables maximum uptime, improved operational efficiency, and long-term reliability.
