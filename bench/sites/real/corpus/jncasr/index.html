Official website of Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research

Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research (JNCASR) is a multidisciplinary research institute situated in Jakkur, a locality north of Bangalore, India. It is relatively young yet well-known around the Globe.

Our mandate is to pursue and promote world-class research and training at the frontiers of Science and Engineering covering broad areas ranging from Materials to Genetics.

It provides a vibrant academic ambience hosting more than 500 Researchers. The Centre is an autonomous institution under Department of Science and Technology, Govt. of India and a deemed to be university.

Academics

With faculty and students from diverse areas of science and engineering, the research undertaken at JNCASR is varied and interdisciplinary in nature. JNCASR draws students into its Ph.D., Integrated Ph.D. and M. Sc. programmes from across the length and breadth of the country. Students are admitted in the August and January semesters and their selection is through a highly competitive process. Our programmes are designed to enable the students to meet the challenges of the future society. At JNCASR, students enjoy the benefit of active discussions on their research areas in the form of annual work presentations.

Research

Being an academic institution of higher learning, the primary goal of JNCASR is to produce well trained Ph.D. and Master of Science students. The research at JNCASR is highly interdisciplinary and collaborative in nature, often leading to translational activities involving industries as well as incubation of startups. Besides core researchers, visiting scientists, teachers and students also contribute in its activities. Research at the Centre is carried out in Units which are divided based on primary research focus.
